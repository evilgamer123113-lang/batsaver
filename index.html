FAQ

    
Which platforms are supported?
BatSaver is a Pinterest video saver, YouTube video saver, TikTok video saver, Instagram video saver, X/Twitter video saver, Reddit video saver — plus any website with standard video/image tags.

    
Does the download have a watermark?
No. It downloads the original file directly from the platform's servers.

    
Will it work on my phone?
Yes — iOS Safari, Chrome on Android, and all modern mobile browsers.

    
The video plays instead of downloading — what do I do?
On a computer, right-click and choose "Save video as…". On iPhone, hold the video and tap "Save Video". On Android, tap the ⋮ menu and choose "Download".

    
Why does YouTube only give me a thumbnail sometimes?
YouTube now encrypts its video streams, so a free static tool can't reliably grab the file. When that happens you'll see the thumbnail with an "Open in YouTube" link.

    
Can I download private videos?
Only public content can be downloaded. Private or restricted content requires the owner's permission.
